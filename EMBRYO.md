# Embryo Browser Archive

Embryo Browser Archive is Embryo-Zero's fork of [browsertrix-crawler](https://github.com/webrecorder/browsertrix-crawler) (v1.15.4, commit 7ac9f64). It does high-fidelity browser-based archiving.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE, NOTICE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
